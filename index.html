About Our Eyelid Surgery

Understanding Eyelid Surgery Covered Insurance

Eyelid surgery, also called blepharoplasty, removes or repositions excess skin, fat and muscle from the upper or lower eyelids. For many patients in Westchester, it refreshes a tired look. For others, it treats a real medical concern.

When sagging upper eyelid skin blocks part of your field of vision, an eyelid lift may be considered medically necessary. In those cases, many insurance plans may help cover part of the cost. Purely cosmetic procedures are generally not covered.

Eyelid Surgery Covered Insurance: FAQs

Quick answers to what patients ask us most about eyelid lift coverage.

Is eyelid surgery covered by insurance?

It can be. Eyelid surgery covered insurance applies mainly when excess upper eyelid skin interferes with your vision and the procedure is deemed medically necessary. Cosmetic-only eyelid surgery is generally not covered. Coverage varies by insurer and plan.

What does insurance usually require for an eyelid lift?

Insurers commonly ask for a medical evaluation, photographs of your eyelids and a visual field test showing that drooping skin affects your sight. Requirements differ between plans, so check with your provider and our team.

Does insurance cover lower eyelid surgery?

Lower eyelid surgery to reduce under-eye bags is usually considered cosmetic, so it is typically not covered. Exceptions are uncommon and depend on a documented medical reason and your plan's rules.

Can I combine a covered procedure with cosmetic work?

Some patients choose to address medical and cosmetic concerns together. In that case, insurance may only cover the medically necessary portion, while the cosmetic portion is paid by the patient. We can explain how this works during your consultation.

How do I find out if my plan covers blepharoplasty?

Call the member services number on your insurance card and ask about blepharoplasty or eyelid surgery benefits and pre-authorization. You can also call us at 914-240-7123 and we will help you know what to ask.
